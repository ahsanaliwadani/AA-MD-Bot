💡 Tips

About broadcast messages:

  • ✅ Messages are sent to the owner's self-chat for each session.
  • ✅ Only connected sessions receive messages.
  • ✅ Disconnected sessions are automatically skipped.
  • ✅ Images are supported — attach any JPG/PNG/WEBP.
  • ⚠️ Large broadcasts may take a few seconds.
  • ⚠️ Don't spam — wait for result before sending again.
